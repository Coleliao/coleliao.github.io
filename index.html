About Me

 My name is Kuo Liao (廖阔), and now I'm a PhD student at Karolinska Institutet (KI), Stockholm, Sweden.
 Currently, my research interests focus on applying multi-omics techniques to explore the development, evolution, and disease of central neural systems (CNS), e.g. amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) and spinal cord injury. Besides, I also have a strong interest in a new regnerative animal model, named spiny mouse (Acomys spp.).
 In terms of technical means, I am interested in the application and data analysis of single-cell sequencing and spatial transcriptomics. Moreover, machine learning and developing new tools for biological data analysis and visualization are also one of my focus.

Research Interests

CNS Evolution

(1) The visual pathway evolution, including the comparation from retina to optic tectum (or say superior colliculus in mammals) and visual cortex (mainly V1 in mammals and DVR or Wulst in sauropsids).
(2) The structural and functional evolution of the mammalian and sauropsid telencephalon.
(3) The genomics evolution of spiny mouse and its neural regnerative capabities.

Brain Development

(1) Neuronal and glial migration and differentiation.
(2) Formation of the brain vascular system, including the neurovascular unit construction and blood–brain barrier formation.
(3) Persistence of neural stemness in adulthood, particularly for adult neurogenesis after injury in new non-model organisms.

CNS Disease

(1) Neurodegenerative diseases, like ALS and MS, expecially for the vascular dysfunction in CNS.
(2) Fibrosis and repair in spinal cord injury. Mainly focus on the difference between house mice and spiny mice.

Tools Development

(1) ML and DL tools for comparison in cell perturbation in scRNA-seq data.
(2) Data quality control and improvement for the segmented cells in spatial omics.
